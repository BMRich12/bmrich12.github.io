3am Dump: Privacy policy

This policy covers the 3am Dump app for iPhone, published by Brody Richmond. It was written from what the app's own code does, and it changes when the code does.

What the app collects

Nothing leaves your device. The app keeps user content on your device only, and deleting the app deletes it.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is asked
MicrophoneSo you can speak your thought instead of typing it. Nothing is recorded — no audio ever leaves the microphone or reaches storage.
Speech recognitionYour words are turned into text on this device and written straight into the field. No audio is saved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Each is asked only when the feature that needs it is used, and the app works without it.

Purchases

The app has no purchases, so no payment data is handled by it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask what personal information the app holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and opt out of any sale or sharing of it (the app sells and shares nothing, and holds nothing off your device). Write to the address below and you will get an answer within 45 days. You will not be treated differently for asking.

Changes and contact
